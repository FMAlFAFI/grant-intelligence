# proposal-to-output drift

Generated by `python -m app.drift.drift_calculator` on 2026-11-11 from `data/processed/pseudonymised` with `Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B@97b0c614be4d`.

**How to read this.** Drift is a measured distance between what was proposed and what was published. It is not a finding about the quality of the work or about any researcher: a project that discovered something other than what it set out to study may have done excellent work. The Research Unit interprets the distance.

**Linkage.** Papers are the ones each final report lists (self-reported). Stage 3 confirmation is not available for them: their DOIs are placeholders written by the synthetic generator, not registered identifiers. Every figure here therefore rests on 300 self-reported links. With real data, the OpenAlex confirmation rate belongs in this paragraph.

**How far the cut-offs can be trusted.** They were calibrated in Experiment 4
(`docs/drift-calibration.md`). On held-out labelled sets they agree with the labels at kappa 0.20 (95% CI -0.02 to 0.42) for the abstract comparison and 0.41 (0.09 to 0.70) for expected outputs.
That is weak agreement. Treat a class as a prompt to look, and always read results by language pairing:
an Arabic proposal compared with an English paper scores lower than a same-language pair at the same
alignment.
